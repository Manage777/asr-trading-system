Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 14, 2026.

How information is handled when you visit the ASR website, register beta interest or contact support.

Scope and privacy contact

ASR Trading System is the name used for this software project and website. This notice covers the current informational website and inquiry process. The site does not currently offer customer accounts, payment processing or checkout. Send privacy questions to the person responsible for ASR privacy inquiries at support@asrtradingsystem.com.

Information involved

  • Beta interest: your name, email address, NinjaTrader experience, optional testing interests, consent wording and the form/policy version accompanying your submission.
  • Support: your email correspondence and any screenshots, software versions, error messages or other details you choose to provide.
  • Technical information: hosting, email and form-verification providers may process IP addresses, browser/device details, timestamps and delivery or security logs as part of their services.

Please remove account numbers and unrelated personal information from attachments. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or trading-account credentials. The beta form does not request financial information.

Why information is used

Information is used to answer the request you make, acknowledge a submission, assess beta interest, communicate relevant participation details, troubleshoot reported issues and protect the inquiry process from abuse. A beta inquiry is not permission for unrelated promotional campaigns. Any broader marketing subscription would need its own appropriate consent process.

Services used today

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the public website and may process technical information when pages are requested. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • FormSubmit: processes form submissions, sends them to ASR’s support mailbox and sends the acknowledgement to the address entered. See its privacy notice and service documentation.
  • Google reCAPTCHA: the FormSubmit verification step uses reCAPTCHA to help prevent automated abuse. Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms apply to that service.
  • Namecheap Private Email: hosts the ASR support mailbox receiving inquiries. See Namecheap’s privacy policy.

The website’s public source repository is not the destination for submitted inquiry details. Avoid putting sensitive information into the optional message because a copy of the submission may be sent to the email address you enter.

Cookies and international processing

The current ASR page code does not include advertising pixels or a visitor-analytics script. This does not mean all providers are cookie-free: the external form and verification services may use cookies or related technologies. Browser settings may restrict these, but doing so can affect verification. You can contact ASR by email instead of using the form.

Service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, where privacy protections and lawful-access rules may differ. Their linked policies describe their own processing arrangements. This notice does not promise that information stays in Canada.

Sharing and disclosure

ASR does not sell inquiry information or provide the beta-interest list to unrelated advertisers. Information is handled through the service providers described above and may be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to address abuse, security incidents or legal claims. Identifiable feedback, testimonials or screenshots will not be published by ASR without separate permission.

Retention and deletion

Retention is based on the purpose of the record: responding to an open inquiry, considering an active beta request, resolving a reported issue, documenting consent or withdrawal, or meeting a legal obligation. Records that are no longer needed for those purposes should be deleted or anonymized. A request to withdraw from beta consideration can be made at any time.

FormSubmit’s documentation currently describes a 30-day submission archive. That provider period does not delete copies already delivered to ASR’s mailbox or to you. Email records, backups and provider logs can have different retention periods; deletion is not necessarily immediate across every system. Contact ASR to request deletion of the records it controls. Limited records may need to be retained to document a withdrawal or comply with applicable obligations.

Your choices and requests

You may ask for access to or correction of information you supplied, request deletion, withdraw from beta communications or raise a privacy concern. Email support@asrtradingsystem.com, preferably from the address used for your inquiry. Include enough information to locate the record; do not send identity documents unless specifically necessary and requested through an appropriate process.

Requests are handled manually. ASR may need to verify that a request concerns your information and will consider it under applicable law, including any retention requirements or exceptions. Withdrawing from beta communications means ASR should no longer contact you about participation, apart from handling your request or necessary legal communications.

Security and audience

No website, email system or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Send only information needed for your inquiry. If you suspect unintended disclosure involving an ASR inquiry, contact support with the relevant details but do not forward exposed credentials.

The beta-interest process is intended for adults who are at least 18 and have reached the age of majority where they live. If information about a minor was submitted, contact ASR to request its removal.

Changes

The date above identifies this version. This notice will be revised when information-handling practices change. New uses requiring additional consent will not be authorized merely by changing this page. Software licensing, diagnostics and paid services will need appropriate disclosures before they are introduced.

Contact

For questions about this page, email support@asrtradingsystem.com. For practical help, visit ASR Support.
