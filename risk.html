Important limitations of futures trading, automation and development or simulation examples.

Substantial risk of loss

Futures trading is not suitable for everyone. Leverage can magnify losses, and an investor may lose all or more than the initial investment. Only risk capital—money that can be lost without jeopardizing financial security or lifestyle—should be used. Software does not remove market risk.

No promised outcome

ASR is being developed for chart analysis, configurable execution and trade management. No software feature, signal, indicator, setting or example guarantees profits, prevents losses or establishes suitability for a particular person. Website information is not personalized investment, financial, legal or tax advice.

Stops, targets and limits

Stops, trailing stops, targets and other controls cannot guarantee execution at a specified price or a maximum loss. Gaps, rapid movement, liquidity, slippage, rejected orders and technical failures may produce different outcomes. A control may apply only to the strategy, instrument or account scope stated in its operating instructions; do not assume account-wide protection.

Automation and supervision

Automation depends on configuration, market data, platform operation and order handling. Incorrect settings, delayed or duplicate actions, software defects, disconnections and broker or exchange issues can affect trades. Understand the specific build and validate behavior in simulation before considering live use. Software controls do not replace supervision or access to your platform’s and broker’s order-management procedures.

If an order or position needs immediate attention, use your platform and broker’s support channels. ASR email support is not an emergency trading service.

Simulation and performance examples

Hypothetical, backtested and playback results do not represent actual trading. They may benefit from hindsight and may not fully account for liquidity, actual fills, slippage, commissions, data quality, changing market conditions or the effects of financial risk on decisions.

Past results do not reliably predict future outcomes. Development screenshots illustrate the interface and may show simulated activity or values; they are not verified evidence of live returns. A favorable example does not establish expected performance or a typical customer result.

Third-party accounts and costs

Brokers, data providers, platforms and funding providers set their own rules, fees and restrictions. ASR compatibility does not establish compliance with a particular provider’s automation, trading or account rules. Verify those requirements independently. Software fees, market-data charges, commissions and other costs can affect overall outcomes.

ASR remains in private-beta preparation. Features, supported configurations, installation and licensing behavior are subject to ongoing validation. A screenshot, successful compilation or individual test does not establish complete customer readiness.
