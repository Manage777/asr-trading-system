Choose the right starting point.

Product or beta questions

Tell us which part of ASR you want to understand and your familiarity with NinjaTrader. Registering interest does not create an account, reserve a license or guarantee an invitation.

Installation or licensing questions

Include the ASR build or package name, your NinjaTrader version and the exact error message. If you have not been invited, there is no customer download or activation process to complete yet.

Report a software issue

  1. Describe the steps that led to the issue and what you expected to happen.
  2. Include the ASR build, NinjaTrader version, instrument, bar configuration and whether you used simulation or playback.
  3. For playback, include the date, time and time zone so the event can be located.
  4. Attach relevant error text and a screenshot with account numbers and personal details removed.

Never send passwords, payment details or trading-account credentials. Do not post license or device identifiers publicly.

What happens after registration?

An automatic email acknowledges your interest submission. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive. Selected testers will receive participation details after validation; the acknowledgement itself is not an invitation.

Understand the current scope.

ASR is software for chart analysis and configurable trade execution. This website does not provide a brokerage account, hold trading funds or offer a mobile trading terminal.

Email support is not an emergency order-management service. If an order or position needs immediate attention, use your trading platform and broker’s support channels.

Response hours and commercial support terms have not yet been announced. We are not promising 24/5 availability or a fixed response time during preparation.
