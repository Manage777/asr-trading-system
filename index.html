02 — EXECUTION ARCHITECTURE

Multiple ways to engage.
One control layer.

The ASR Execution Engine is designed around selective execution and user-defined risk. Different models evaluate different market behaviours while the trader retains authority over configuration and trade management.

AUTO AReversal + PullbackEvaluates potential turning points near structural levels, exhaustion and qualifying pullback behaviour.
AUTO BMomentumEvaluates directional momentum and trend-continuation conditions.
AUTO CInside-Out ContinuationUses ASR price-bar structure and second-bar continuation conditions for configurable entries.

Auto A, B and C are under private-beta validation. Signals, stops, targets and order handling must be tested in simulation before any live use. Proprietary entry calculations are not published.

08 — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Know what you're getting.

Is ASR available to purchase today?

Not yet. ASR is preparing for limited private-beta testing. Public availability and pricing will be announced only after validation and vendor onboarding are complete.

Which trading platform does ASR use?

ASR is being developed for NinjaTrader 8 Desktop, with a focus on futures trading.

Can I trade manually as well as automatically?

Yes. The ASR execution design includes manual controls and configurable Auto A, Auto B and Auto C modes. All automated behaviour requires careful simulation testing.

Does ASR guarantee profitable trades?

No. ASR provides software tools, not guaranteed outcomes. Futures trading can result in substantial losses, and simulated results may differ from live trading.

How will licenses work?

Licensing integration is under validation. Device restrictions, customer activation and renewal procedures will be confirmed before customer release.
